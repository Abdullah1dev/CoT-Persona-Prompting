# Chain-of-Thought + Persona Prompt

## Business Scenario

A small software company has $50,000 available for one major investment this year.

The company has three options:

1. Spend the money on marketing to attract more customers.
2. Hire two additional developers to speed up product development.
3. Improve the existing product by investing in performance, reliability, and user experience.

The company currently has a small development team, receives some customer complaints about product performance, and has experienced slower customer growth during the last six months.

The company wants to increase revenue while maintaining customer satisfaction and keeping the business financially stable.

## Prompt

You are a senior business strategy consultant with expertise in technology companies, product strategy, and business growth.

Think through the problem step-by-step before providing your final recommendation.

Analyze the company's current situation, identify the most important factors affecting the decision, and evaluate the potential benefits, risks, costs, and long-term impact of each option.

Compare the three options carefully and consider how each one could affect revenue growth, customer satisfaction, product quality, development capacity, and financial stability.

Then provide a final recommendation and explain why it is the most suitable choice for this company.

Present your answer in a clear and structured format.
