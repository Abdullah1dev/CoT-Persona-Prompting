# Conclusion

This experiment demonstrated that prompt design can significantly influence the quality and structure of an AI-generated response. The plain prompt produced a correct and useful recommendation, while the Chain-of-Thought + Persona prompt provided a more structured, detailed, and business-focused analysis.

The **Senior Business Strategy Consultant** persona helped the AI approach the problem from a professional business perspective, while the reasoning instruction encouraged it to consider multiple factors such as customer satisfaction, revenue growth, product quality, and financial stability before making a recommendation.

Interestingly, both approaches reached the same final conclusion: **the company should prioritize improving its existing product**. This shows that CoT + Persona does not necessarily change the final answer, but it can improve the **depth, organization, clarity, and usefulness of the analysis**.

Overall, the experiment showed that effective prompt engineering is not only about asking an AI a question, but also about providing the right **role, context, reasoning guidance, and output expectations** to produce more useful results.
