Plain Prompt — Result

The company should prioritize improving the existing product, particularly its performance, reliability, and user experience.

1. Marketing

Advantages:

Can attract new customers and increase market visibility.
May generate additional sales in the short term.
Helps the company reach a larger potential customer base.

Disadvantages:

Marketing may bring in more customers without solving the existing product-performance complaints.
New customers could have a poor experience if product problems remain.
The investment may not generate the expected return if customer retention is weak.
2. Hiring Two Additional Developers

Advantages:

Increases the company's development capacity.
Allows the team to develop new features more quickly.
Reduces pressure on the existing development team.
Could help the company support future growth.

Disadvantages:

A significant portion of the $50,000 could be spent on salaries and onboarding.
New developers may take time to understand the existing product and codebase.
Hiring alone does not guarantee that current performance and reliability issues will be solved.
3. Improving the Existing Product

Advantages:

Directly addresses the customer complaints about product performance.
Better reliability and user experience can increase customer satisfaction.
A better product can improve customer retention and potentially encourage positive recommendations.
Solving existing problems creates a stronger foundation for future marketing and growth.

Disadvantages:

The company may not see immediate revenue growth.
Product improvements require development time and careful prioritization.
Spending the full budget on improvements could limit the company's ability to invest in marketing or hiring.
Final Recommendation

The company should invest the $50,000 in improving the existing product.

The most important reason is that the company already has evidence of product-performance problems and slowing customer growth. Spending heavily on marketing before addressing these problems could attract new customers to a product that currently has quality issues, potentially increasing dissatisfaction and hurting retention.

Improving performance, reliability, and user experience first would create a stronger product and help protect customer satisfaction. Once the product is more stable and reliable, the company could consider increasing marketing investment to drive customer growth.

Recommended priority:

Improve the existing product → stabilize customer experience → then scale customer acquisition.
